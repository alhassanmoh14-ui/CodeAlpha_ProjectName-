
# PHISHING EXAMPLES AND ANALYSIS

These examples are fictional training scenarios.

## Example 1: Fake university email

Subject: Urgent — Student Account Suspension

Message:
Your student email account will be disabled today.
Click the link in this message to prevent suspension.

Warning signs:
- Unnecessary urgency.
- Threat of account suspension.
- Unexpected login link.

Safe action:
Visit the official university portal independently.

## Example 2: Fake delivery message

Message:
Your parcel is waiting. Pay a redelivery fee immediately.

Warning signs:
- Unexpected payment request.
- Unknown link.
- Pressure to act immediately.

Safe action:
Check the delivery through the official company's website or application.

## Example 3: Fake bank call

A caller claims to be a bank employee and requests your one-time verification code.

Warning signs:
- Request for confidential information.
- Unverified caller identity.
- Pressure to provide the code.

Safe action:
Never disclose verification codes. End the call and contact your bank through its official number.

## Example 4: Business Email Compromise

An employee receives an email requesting a change to a supplier's bank details.

Warning signs:
- Unexpected payment instructions.
- Urgent request.
- Changes to financial information.

Safe action:
Verify the change using a previously known telephone number and follow internal approval procedures.

## Example 5: Fake prize notification

Message:
Congratulations! You have won a smartphone. Pay a processing fee to claim your prize.

Warning signs:
- Unexpected prize.
- Request for money.
- Pressure to act quickly.

Safe action:
Do not pay or provide personal information. Verify the promotion independently.
